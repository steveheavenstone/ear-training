Scale degree recognition

A cadence establishes the key, then one mystery note plays. Sing the note, walk it to do in your head, commit out loud — then answer. Use Resolve to hear the walk to the tonic.

Set your level, pick a key (or Random), and hit New question.

Chord function ID

Cadence, then one diatonic chord in a two-hand piano voicing. Ask first: is it home (tonic), moving away (subdominant), or pulling home (dominant)? Then name the numeral. On L5 the bass may play the 3rd or 5th — the root crutch is gone.

Hear the changes

Cadence, then a progression — mostly generated fresh from functional-harmony rules, so you can't pattern-match a short list. From L3 up, chords may come in close, drop-2, drop-3, or drop-4 spreads, like a real pianist. Sing the bass line back, then fill in the numerals. Bass replays the bass line alone. On L5 some chords are inverted (shown as I/V, vi/iii …): name the chord, not the bass note.

Melody by numbers

Cadence, then a short phrase. Sing it back on degree numbers first, then enter the degrees in order. Tap Undo to fix a slip.
